# ECHO//FALL — the hand and the record

The world remembers what you did, but not why. Architecture preserves the shape of a gesture; people remember its cost. The opening asks: **Why does this place already know your hand?** No voice explains the answer.

## Characters

The Wanderer is a small, asymmetric witness: cool ceramic mask, dark narrow coat, a single rose scarf breaking the silhouette, and an exposed hand reaching toward light. Keep feet/weapon edges clear. Rose belongs to the living person, cyan to recorded actions, and ember to something taken. Avoid adding generic armor, floating numbers or luminous outlines around every actor. Current model-rendered clips are a motion reference; final work needs an authored mask/coat silhouette and expressive reach, recoil and listening poses.

The practice warden is an obsolete promise still keeping watch: upright, chipped, marked by a split ivory standard. Its white anticipation is a readable commitment; a clean deflect lets that commitment end. Sentinels repeat institutional verticals; Lancers turn them into forward needles; Drones are suspended lenses. King owns symmetry and prediction; Mother owns radial enclosure and borrowed silhouettes; Child should read as unarmored, grounded and vulnerable. Keep all existing combat telegraph colors.

The creature curls around a warm center. Its outline should remain distinct from both humanoids and architecture. The Keeper's weight hangs from the staff and empty cage; the Child's uncovered hands should carry the scene. Their current rendered models remain studies pending bespoke concept and animation work.

## Memory and interface

Use an incomplete ring crossed by two separated strokes: a record that cannot contain a whole life. Use it at glass, title and remembered sites, not as general wallpaper. A living cyan trace and an ember absence must be distinguishable without reading a stat panel. Never reveal a future mechanical consequence in the first choice.

Title: generous negative space, ECHO//FALL wordmark, one sentence of premise. Gameplay: compact one-topic teaching card with input, purpose and acknowledgement. Dialogue: plain sentence case, restrained names, action-based options. Transfer: describe the remembered deed before naming its identity. Existing fallback font stays readable; commission/license one distinctive display face and one highly readable body face before final typography sign-off.

## Sound and pacing

Wake should breathe before it sings: low air, a remote signal and small physical contact. Use the existing palette's quiet signal for recognition and transfer cue for crossing; avoid a reward fanfare for moral decisions. Material footsteps remain subtle. Final production needs close foley, a creature breath, a warden release, and longer evolving regional arrangements. The current synthesized library is functional authored placeholder sound design, not final audio approval.

## Opening rhythm

Arrival → safe movement/first ledge → warden's promise → wounded creature → return to listening glass → choose the deed to carry → changed place → optional routes through the Belfry/Cistern/Archive. All controls stay available. Lessons can be skipped and revisited. The first loop can be short; the surrounding exploration supports the 10–15 minute target without artificial waiting or new locks.
